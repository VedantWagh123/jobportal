# References (Project Sources & Bibliography)

**"References"** ka matlab kya hota hai?
Jab aap koi bada project banate ho, toh jury aapse puchti hai ki "Aapne ye idea kahan se liya? Aapne kaunsi technology aur research padh ke isko banaya?" 

References section mein aapko un sabhi websites, research papers, aur documentation ki list deni hoti hai jinki madad se aapne apna project complete kiya. Ye prove karta hai ki aapka project hawa mein nahi bana hai, balki solid research aur official documents par based hai.

Aapko apne PPT ke aakhri (last) slide par **"References"** ki list lagani hoti hai. Niche aapke project ke hisaab se ekdum accurate aur professional references diye gaye hain.
